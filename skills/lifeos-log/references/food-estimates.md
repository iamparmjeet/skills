# Food Estimates

Rough per-portion values for Indian vegetarian home food, based on IFCT 2017 / USDA-style averages. They are planning estimates, not lab values. A product label always wins (pea protein, pre-workout, packaged namkeen). Oil and ghee are the biggest hidden variable: add ~45 kcal per teaspoon when the user mentions it, or when the sabji is visibly oily.
