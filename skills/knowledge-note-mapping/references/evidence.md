# Evidence Discipline — Reference

Preserve the source's position and assess it in parallel. Do not silently replace a creator's claim with a summary.

## Steps per substantive/high-stakes claim

1. Record the claim faithfully and specifically, with timestamp/section for audio/video.
2. State independent verification beside it: supported | partly supported | unsupported | contested | not yet verified.
3. Link to strongest suitable authority:
   - Medical: systematic reviews, clinical guidelines (not credential alone)
   - Technical: governing standard/spec (RFC), then official docs, then reproducible test
   - Political/historical/legal/financial: authoritative primary sources, official data
4. State reason for any disagreement: population, dose, timeframe, outcome, study quality, uncertainty.
5. Promote only the qualified, independently supported conclusion into a reusable atomic note. Keep creator's framing and unresolved questions in source/reference note.

## Compact comparison (use when it clarifies)

```
Source claim: …
Independent verification: …
Assessment: supported | partly supported | unsupported | contested | not yet verified
Limits: …
```

## Rules

- Treat a source's interpretation as interpretation unless independently verified.
- Keep background/orientation sources distinct from evidence for substantive claims.
- Separate domestic causes, foreign involvement, and foreign orchestration. Relationship/influence/membership ≠ proof of control.
- Replace vague labels with testable propositions: actor, action, channel, time, evidenced outcome.
- Preserve uncertainty explicitly; do not turn a broad phrase or slogan into conclusion without evidence.
